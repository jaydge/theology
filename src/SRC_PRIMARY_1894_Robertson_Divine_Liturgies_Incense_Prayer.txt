p. 217 (English facing the Greek):

    "And the Priest saith the Prayer of the incense:

        Incense we offer to thee, O Christ our God, for an odour of a spiritual
        sweet smell: which accepting at thy heavenly Altar, in return send down
        on us the grace of thine All-holy Spirit."

ALTAR CLAUSE: "at thy heavenly Altar" — "Altar" capitalised, "heavenly"
  lower-case; the verb is a participle, "which accepting".
