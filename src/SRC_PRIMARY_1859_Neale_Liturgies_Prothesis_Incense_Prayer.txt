Neale prints "The Order of the Holy Prothesis, as performed in the Great Church,
and the Holy Mountain" as a distinct section beginning at p. 161, separate from
the Liturgy of S. Chrysostom proper.

p. 169, rubric and prayer as printed (small caps as in the original):

    "The Priest saith the Prayer of Incense.

        We offer to Thee incense, O CHRIST our GOD, for a savour of a spiritual
        perfume: receive it unto Thy heavenly altar, and send down in its stead
        the grace of Thy most HOLY SPIRIT."

ALTAR CLAUSE: "receive it unto Thy heavenly altar" — lower-case "altar";
  verb "receive" in the imperative.

Neale's own note states that his Prothesis text is "Slightly altered from my
Introduction," i.e. from the General Introduction to "A History of the Holy
Eastern Church." That volume was not separately verified in this pass.
