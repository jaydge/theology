p. 25 (leaf 65), general chapter on ceremonial actions, "putting on incense":

    "While putting on the incense the celebrant says *Ab illo benedicaris in
    cuius honore cremaberis. Amen.* Only on one occasion, at the offertory in
    Mass, is there another formula, *Per intercessionem beati Michaelis
    archangeli*, etc., as in the missal. Then he hands the spoon back to the
    deacon or M.C. … The celebrant makes the sign of the cross over the thurible,
    saying nothing."

p. 121 (leaf 161), Solemn Mass, the incensing at the beginning:

    "…then puts incense into the thurible three times with the spoon, saying
    *Ab illo benedicaris in cuius honore cremaberis. Amen*, after the deacon has
    said *Benedicite pater reverende*. … This is the invariable way in which
    incense is blessed."

SPELLING/PUNCTUATION VARIANT WORTH LOGGING: Fortescue prints "cuius"; Gihr and
  the 19th-century missals print "cujus". Fortescue prints the formula with NO
  comma after "benedicaris".

SEARCH THAT CAME UP EMPTY: "altaris incensi" — 0 hits in Fortescue, confirming
  he never prints the offertory formula in full.

PUBLISHED ENGLISH FOR "AB ILLO BENEDICARIS" — use Gihr, not Fortescue:

Nicholas Gihr, "The Holy Sacrifice of the Mass," B. Herder, 1902, p. 376
(PUBLIC DOMAIN):
    "Be thou blessed by Him in whose honor thou wilt be consumed. Amen."

An unattributed modern congregational booklet, "Traditional Latin Mass MISSAL —
Latin-English Ordinary of the Mass of the Roman Rite, 1962"
(https://maternalheart.org/library/missal.pdf), renders it:
    "Be blessed by Him in whose honour thou art burnt. Amen."
    — NO translator, publisher, or date appears on the file. Cite only as an
    unattributed modern rendering, never as a published translation. Its
    Ordinary does NOT contain the four offertory incensation prayers (no match
    for "Michaelis", "Accendat", "Dirigatur", "Incensum istud").

PRE-TRIDENTINE WITNESS FOR "PER INTERCESSIONEM":
archive.org item missaleromanumme01cath (Henry Bradshaw Society edition of the
Milan 1474 Missale Romanum) prints it with a COLON where later books have a
comma:
    "Per intercessionem beati michaelis archangeli stantis a dextris altaris
    incensi : et omnium electorum suorum incensum istud dignetur dominus
    bene(✠)dicere. et in odorem suauitatis accipere. Per christum dominum
    nostrum. amen."
    [Note the "." (not ":") after "benedicere".]
