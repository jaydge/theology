(1) Latin:
    "Per intercessionem beati Michaelis archangeli stantis a dextris altaris
    incensi, et omnium electorum suorum, incensum istud dignetur Dominus
    benedicere, et in odorem suavitatis accipere. Per Christum Dominum nostrum.
    Amen."
    [OCR runs words together: "Michaelisarchangelistantis", "altarisincensi",
     "pnostrum".]

    English:
    "By the intercession of blessed Michael the archangel standing on the right
    hand of the altar of incense, and of all his elect, may the Lord vouchsafe
    to bless this incense, and receive it as an odour of sweetness. Through
    Christ our Lord. Amen."

(2) Latin:
    "Incensum istud a te benedictum, ascendat ad te Domine, et descendat super
    nos misericordia tua."
    [No comma after "istud"; comma after "benedictum"; comma — not colon —
     after "Domine".]

    English:
    "May this incense blessed by thee, ascend to thee, O Lord, and may thy mercy
    descend upon us."

(3) Latin:
    "Dirigatur, Domine, oratio mea, sicut incensum, in conspectu tuo; elevatio
    manuum mearum sacrificium vespertinum. Pone, Domine, custodiam ori meo, et
    ostium circumstantiae labiis meis: ut non declinet cor meum in verba
    malitiae, ad excusandas excusationes in peccatis."
    [SEMICOLON after "conspectu tuo", not colon. OCR: "circumstantis",
     "deelinet", "malitiz".]

    English:
    "Let my prayer be directed, O Lord, as incense, in thy sight; the lifting up
    of my hands as even sacrifice. Set a watch, O Lord, before my mouth: and a
    door round about my lips: that my heart may not incline to evil words: to
    make excuses in sins."

(4) Latin:
    "Accendat in nobis Domi[nus]…"   [OCR truncated at the column break.]

    English:
    "May the Lord enkindle / within us the fire of his love, and the flame of
    eternal charity. Amen."

     38 -> leaf 39); the join is at "enkindle | within us". Confident of the
     reading, but this is a two-snippet reconstruction, NOT a single verbatim
     capture.]
