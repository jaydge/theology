8. ⭐ **THE VINTAGE PAIR CONVENTION (`VP-`), adopted 260728-1 from patch `260727-2` §0.4.** *State only; the rationale lives in the patch and in `St_Francis_EMC_Distinctives.md`.*

   **What it is for:** the corpus now holds material from materially different periods. **A 2022 position is not a current position, and it is not superseded by silence either.** Without a convention the two get merged, and merging them is the DQ-5 defect — one fact held in two places, drifting.

   **Format:**
   ```
   ⏳ VINTAGE PAIR VP-N — <the question, stated once>
   ```

   ⭐ **THE `DEEP HISTORY` CELL, ADOPTED 260729-1 (Ruling 2, project-wide, not batch-local).** A `VP-` block **may** carry one optional cell **above `THEN`**, for material **earlier than the block's existing `THEN`**. Four points, and the fourth is the one that will be got wrong:
   1. ⚠️ **It is OPTIONAL.** A block without one is not incomplete.
   2. It exists so that a finding older than the current `THEN` can be recorded **without overwriting or renumbering the `THEN` cell**, which rule 1 and the never-alter rule both forbid.
   3. **It carries the same `FIND:` obligation as every other cell** (rule 2) wherever the source is greppable. ⚠️ **Where the source is held outside the repo (the `BLOG` class, §0), say so in the cell rather than supplying a string that cannot be run.**
   4. ⛔ **`DELTA` STILL COMPARES `THEN` AND `NOW`, AND ONLY THOSE TWO.** A `DEEP HISTORY` cell **does not change what `DELTA` measures and never upgrades or implies anything about `DELTA` on its own.** ⚠️ **A third dated point in an older register makes rule 3's reasons stronger, not weaker:** more vintage is more distance, not more confirmation. **An unasked question is still not an unchanged position.**

   **Three rules, and they are the whole convention:**
   1. ⚠️ **A VINTAGE PAIR LIVES IN EXACTLY ONE PLACE** — in `St_Francis_EMC_Distinctives.md`, **beside the newer finding.** Other documents point at it and never restate it. **This is the DQ-5 rule applied to vintage.**
   2. ⚠️ **`FIND:` IS MANDATORY ON BOTH SIDES AND MUST BE A STRING THAT ACTUALLY GREPS.** Byte offsets rot when a capture is replaced; a verbatim string survives re-transcription and re-capture. Four to ten words, distinctive, **ASR quirks preserved exactly as transcribed.** ⛔ **Never supply a `FIND:` from memory — run the grep.**
   3. ⚠️ **`DELTA: UNKNOWN` IS LEGITIMATE AND COMMON AND MUST NOT BE GUESSED INTO CHANGED OR UNCHANGED. An unasked question is not an unchanged position.** ⛔ **A `DELTA: CHANGED` may never be put to him as a change until both sides are verified** — *"you used to say X"* is the most relationship-costly sentence available in this project and must never rest on an inference.

An empty cell is neither and is not acceptable output.** *"Not filled"* and *"not on record"* are different facts and the corpus must distinguish them. **An absence is written up `[Analysis]` with NO tag** (rule 3: a corpus gap is not a source), and **it does not consume a finding number.**

9. ⭐ **THE SPLIT QUOTE-LENGTH RULE, ADOPTED 260729-1 (Ruling 3, project-wide, governs all future intake).** ⚠️ **State plainly what this is: the FIRST quote-length rule in the repo.** Before this pass **no quote-length rule existed anywhere** (confirmed by grep). **It replaces nothing and supersedes nothing.**

   | Class of text | Rule |
   |---|---|
   | **Rev. James's own words** — blog prose, papers, transcript, Discord, anything he authored | **Under 15 words per quote · one quote per source maximum** (the project's standing copyright discipline) |
   | **Public-domain 16th/17th-century formulary or Reformation-era text HE is quoting** — Cranmer's *Of Ceremonies*, the Articles, the Homilies, the BCP | **May run fuller.** These are **not his copyrighted prose**, and a tight cap on them destroys real evidence |

   ⚠️ **The split is about copyright, not about importance.** A long Cranmer quotation is cheap to reproduce and is the actual evidence in a ceremonial-authority finding; a long quotation of *his* prose is both a copyright problem and, in this project, an attribution risk. ⛔ **Quoting a formulary at length never licenses quoting his surrounding argument at length — attribute the two separately and keep the layers visible.**

   ⭐⭐ **AMENDED 260729-2 — THE PER-SOURCE COUNT IS REPLACED BY A PROPORTION. THIS IS A CORRECTION OF A RULE ADOPTED ONE PASS EARLIER, NOT A LOOSENING.** ⚠️ **State the reason plainly: rule 9 as written capped quotation at *one quote per source*, and the very pass that adopted it (`260729-1`, BLOG batch 1) produced a ledger that quotes roughly per FINDING.** The rule and its own precedent disagreed from the day the rule was written, and the BLOG batch 2 intake thread declared the tension rather than glossing it (patch §5A). ⛔ **The error is in the rule, not in the practice.** The amended rule, which governs all future intake:

   1. **Any single quotation of his own prose: UNDER 15 WORDS.** Unchanged, and hard.
   2. ⭐ **Per source, cumulative quotation of his own prose stays a SMALL FRACTION of that source** — as a working figure, **under roughly a tenth of its body by word count** — **and must never reproduce the source's structure or let the corpus substitute for reading the original.** ⚠️ **This is the real constraint, and it is PROPORTIONAL, not a count.** A count is the wrong instrument: one quotation from a 400-word post displaces more than four from a 17,000-word one.
   3. **Within (1) and (2), quote only what is needed to anchor a finding.** **Paraphrase by default; prefer `FIND:` locators to additional quotation.**
   4. **Public-domain formulary, liturgical or patristic text that HE quotes** (Prayer Book, Articles, Homilies, Cranmer, the Fathers) **may run fuller**, is **attributed to its own source and never to him**, and ⛔ **does not count toward (2).**
   5. ⚠️ **`FIND:` locators under five words are VERIFICATION ANCHORS, not quotations**, and do not count toward anything.

   ⛔ **What this does NOT do.** It does not relax (1), it does not license reproducing a post, and it does not apply retroactively as a permission — **it legitimises the practice both batches already followed, which is the point of correcting the rule rather than the ledgers.** ⚠️ **Batch 1's committed findings are not re-trimmed** (never-alter), and batch 2's were checked against (2) at its heaviest source rather than trimmed by count.

### ⭐ 8. ANCHOR BY HEADING TEXT, NEVER BY SECTION NUMBER (standing rule, adopted 260728-2, CL-2)

⚠️ **A patch block emitted in APPEND MODE must anchor each finding by the TARGET HEADING'S TEXT, not by its § number.**

```
Anchor: "Priesthood & Absolution"        ✅ correct
Anchor: §5 (ministry)                    ⛔ wrong, and silently so
```

**Why this is a rule and not a preference.** The `260727-2` patch had **five of eight § anchors wrong by number** (`§2 self-location` → really §1; `§5 ministry` → §11; `§7 formularies` → `§Formularies`; `§11 Rome` → §3). ⛔ **Five of eight is not a slip. It is a process with no feedback loop:** an intake thread in APPEND MODE has **never seen the distinctives**, so it emits numbers it has no way to verify, and the reconcile thread absorbs the error silently by placing findings from the anchor's *description*.

**Two properties make heading text strictly better.** It is **greppable**, so the reconcile thread can verify an anchor mechanically instead of interpreting it. And it is **stable under renumbering** — *a patch that says `Anchor: "Ministry and succession"` cannot be wrong by renumbering, whereas every § number in every open patch goes stale the moment a section is inserted.*

⚠️ **`St_Francis_EMC_Distinctives.md` MIXES NUMBERED AND NAMED SECTIONS** (`## 11. Priesthood & Absolution` alongside `## §Formularies — …`), **so a bare § number is not even well-defined for part of the document.** That alone is sufficient reason.

**Reconcile-side rule:** if a patch arrives with a § number anyway, **place by the description and record the mapping**, per disk-wins — and **correct the emitting prompt**, because an unrecorded mapping is a defect generator that survives the pass.

## 8. RECURRING CURRENCY AUDIT — the checklist

> **⛔ RETIRED 260725-1: the distinctives JSONL re-encode.** The former §8 staged a machine-readable conversion of `St_Francis_EMC_Distinctives.md`. **JD is satisfied with the prose format; the proposal is retired, not deferred.** Do not re-propose it. Its one durable warning is preserved elsewhere and still binds: *never flatten the three-layer attribution system, and never drop the do-not-deploy guards.* That warning now applies to any tooling that touches the corpus, not to a conversion that is not happening.

**Why this replaced it.** The re-encode was a solution to slow retrieval. The failures this project has actually sustained were **currency failures, not retrieval failures**: a stale status carried nine days (DQ-5), a stale item carried three versions (item 20), two validator checks running zero times while reporting clean, three question IDs cited into a void, one tag doing double duty for three months, a §15 that had not moved since the RC batch, and a README a month out of date. Every one of those is a thing that was true once and quietly stopped being true. **This checklist is the instrument for that class.**

**Cadence:** every reconcile pass, and any standalone pass tagged CURRENCY AUDIT. **Run in order.** Items 1-3 are mechanical; 4-8 need judgment and cannot be delegated to the validator.

### 1. Tooling first — did the checks actually run?
- [ ] Run `python3 validate_project.py` (add a root path if not run from the repo root).
- [ ] **Read the COVERAGE SUMMARY before reading the results.** A check reporting **0 files seen is a failure**, regardless of the error count. The run exits non-zero on zero-coverage precisely so this cannot be skimmed past.
- [ ] Confirm every registered path in §4 resolved **exactly**. A `[C0] resolved by search` warning means the registry is wrong; fix the registry, not the file.
- [ ] Remember the pre-commit hook: a silent skip makes every commit since the defect look verified. **Distrust a clean pass you did not read the coverage line of.**

### 2. Stamps and registry

- [ ] Stamps stay machine-parseable. `PROJECT_STATE.md` and `SRC_Manifest.md` were versioned in **prose only** until 260725-1 and were therefore uncheckable by C3; both now carry a standard `**Last updated:**` line. **Do not revert either to a prose-only version note.**

- [ ] Source tags: numbering unbroken, no duplicates, no tag doing double duty (C2). **A tag serving two findings is a citation trail that silently forks.**
- [ ] **Sessions, not hashes** (C12, added 260725-4). Every session row in `SRC_Manifest.md` marked `SECONDARY — SWEEP PENDING` has a recorded sweep; every byte offset cited after 260725 carries a capture code. **A registered-but-unreconciled second capture is the two-copies-of-one-fact condition this file exists to prevent, and it must not sit quietly across commits.**
